# VIRTURA Spaces as Living Media Channels — IP Production Model v0.1

Date: 2026-11-08. Product strategy and publication hypothesis; not a claim that automated content channels, music catalog, social accounts or revenue already exist.

## Core thesis

Every world is a geographically and narratively coherent digital place with its own identity, editor, release calendar and useful experiences. It can publish episodes like an independent media channel, while visitors can actually enter its space through the VIRTURA Space portal. The purpose is not a volume content farm of a still image plus generated music.

World = spatial location + visible continuity + sound ecology + visitor action + archival memory + recurring publication + support/service. Each world is treated as a product with an owner and verification gate.

## A world can publish: standard production architecture

1. WORLD BIBLE: canonical city map and station coordinates (if fictional, say fictional), architecture, object taxonomy, light/weather, color, sound ecology, recurring characters, authorship/rights.
2. CONTENT BRIEF: public promise and one scene/event, user value, target duration, one reproducible input/interaction, narrative continuity with previous release, media license.
3. MEDIA ASSETS: existing original Blender/GN/WebGL/gaussian models and real environment photographs first; new AI-generated backgrounds only after art direction, world geometry and camera match.
4. AUDIO LAB: deliberate composition instruction → agent sketches/research of sound palette → original stems or fully licensed synth/samples → Ableton/Max mix/master/edit → original music rights and loudness report. AI output is a draft, not proof of music quality or ownership.
5. EXPERIENCE: a directly accessible web viewer or embedded player with clear controls, captions, no mandatory headset, audio opt-in, ability to exit/continue, readable updates.
6. RELEASE: publish approved file + source/credit ledger + native poster/frame + version ID + short teaser + scene page + distribution post; record which external platform account actually posted.
7. LEARN: monitor meaningful starts/completions, repeat visits, service inquiries, saved worlds and direct feedback; never invent conversion or subscriber count.

## Proposed formats and realistic first pilot

**Pilot: Green Belt Café / 静水区的一间咖啡馆.** Use one verified space asset and one short original audio work. Three release packages from the same asset set:

- 30–60 second short-form visual scene/teaser, brand-safe and without false 'sleep cure' claims.
- 8–15 minute looping ambient scene with original noise/wind/water/café sound and a meaningful change, not a still image with a random AI song.
- Responsive browser scene: simple panorama or camera loop with mute, volume, captions/metadata, one visual interaction and return to SpacePort. XR is optional future extension.

Output is one package with a consistent station ID and version, not three different products/repositories. First publish a proven package under main SpacePort channel. Only create a separate café social account after at least four quality releases, a consistent schedule and an identified owner/moderator.

**Secondary pilot: Balloon Live.** Start from existing approved audiovisual session recording and build a playlist with cue/section metadata. Do not imply continuous club live streaming until playback rights, hosting and scheduling work.

## Artistic quality gates, not a generative volume contest

- VISUAL: consistent world architecture and camera logic; readable in 16:9, 9:16 and browser; no random extra buildings, characters or recognizable third-party franchises; source media retains aspect ratio.
- AUDIO: original/properly licensed; clean loop seam, avoid clipped peaks, abrupt AI artifacts, repetitive padding; measured loudness and optional headphone-safe start.
- WRITING: one clear fictional location, one tangible user task, concise world lore, no fake dates and claims.
- INTERACTION: page actually loads; one action produces a visible change; exit/return; keyboard/mobile control; accessibility for autoplay and sound.
- LEGAL: original artwork and photography rights checked; artists credited per version; simulated banking/medical/wellness propositions clearly fictional.
- RELEASE: a real accessible URL, test screenshots, roll-back file, release notes and owner. 'Weekly update' only if the editorial queue has an approved real episode.

## Operations and ownership

| Function | Owner | AI use | Approval gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art direction / world continuity | human creative lead | alternative composition and script drafts | final scene design and rights |
| Sound authoring | sound artist/producer | sketches, taxonomy, sound generation | listen-through, mix/master and sample license |
| Editorial / lore | curator/writer | summary, timelines, transcripts | continuity and wording |
| Web product | OpenCode/technical lead | templates, tests, publishing scripts | actual browser and device QA |
| Distribution | designated channel editor | captions, assets, schedule drafts | human approval before each post |
| Service / commerce | Studio lead | package proposals and intake forms | actual delivery, pricing, tax and privacy policy |

**Independent social account is a milestone, not a prerequisite.** Begin with channel pages and playlists under a single accountable VIRTURA account. Separate handle only after quality, ongoing posts and audience needs justify support cost. Same for music streaming/distribution identities.

Choose one (Green Belt Café recommended), deliver one real website/episode package with visual/audio/credit evidence, and only then graduate its world page from archive to playable.
